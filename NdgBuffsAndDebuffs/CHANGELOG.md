# Changelog

## 0.0.1

- First version. Buffs, debuffs and reminders in six freely positionable display groups:
  - Reminders (ultimate ready, potion off cooldown, no food/drink buff), long and short duration buffs, debuffs, target buffs, target debuffs.
  - Per group: on/off, icon/text/both, size, growth direction, text position.
  - Blacklist to hide buffs/debuffs by name; toggles for each reminder.
- Expiring auras linger briefly with a red "0s" before they are removed.
- `/ndgbuffs lock` / `/ndgbuffs unlock` to move the groups.
