# Changelog

## 0.1.0
- First version: a big red "Stop Zerging" warning while you are more than 20 m (configurable) from the group leader.
- Options: only in PvP areas, ignore beyond a distance, show the distance in the text, text size and color, and a movable position (unlock in the settings or with `/ndgurge unlock`).
- Extension for NdgCrown 1.1.0: uses its leader tracking, and its settings are a submenu in NdgCrown's settings panel.
